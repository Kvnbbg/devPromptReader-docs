# 05e — RSS / Atom Feed Parsing, Home & Mobile Navigation, and Symbiosis

## Investigation of Available Feeds

As of the documentation date, the Tech & Stream home surface advertises the following discovery links (extracted from the document head):

- `application/rss+xml` — `/rss.xml` (Blog & Sport)
- `application/atom+xml` — `/atom.xml` (Blog & Sport)
- `application/rss+xml` — `/blog/rss.xml`
- `application/atom+xml` — `/blog/atom.xml`
- `application/rss+xml` — `/sport/rss.xml`
- `application/atom+xml` — `/sport/atom.xml`

These feeds are publicly reachable, return well-formed RSS 2.0 / Atom content, and include standard channel and item metadata (title, link, description, pubDate, categories, optional media enclosures).

No dedicated feeds under the exact paths `/savoirs` or `/dev` were confirmed at the time of investigation; content relevant to those conceptual nodes appears within the general blog and home feed surfaces. Implementers should treat the discovered feeds as the authoritative dry sources and re-verify discovery links periodically.

## Parsing Requirements

- Prefer native or well-maintained libraries that correctly handle both RSS 2.0 and Atom 1.0.
- Respect `Cache-Control`, `ETag`, and `Last-Modified` headers for efficient polling.
- Sanitise all extracted HTML descriptions before display.
- Limit the number of items rendered in any single view to preserve mobile performance and single-column layout integrity.
- All external item links opened from the reader must use `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"` and accessible new-tab indication.

## Integration Points

### Home Surface
The home page already surfaces a “Derniers du feed” section and an “Ouvrir le feed” entry point. Suggestions generated by the skippable-returning mechanism may draw directly from the same feed items, ensuring consistency of content provenance.

### Mobile Navigation
Under the 375 px base and stacked-layout rules, feed-derived suggestions and navigation entries must collapse into the single-column mobile navigation pattern. Touch targets remain ≥ 44 px. No horizontal scroll of feed lists is permitted.

### devPromptReader Symbiosis
Feed items may appear inside the reader as:

- optional knowledge cards unlocked by progression vectors;
- content for the skippable returning suggestion;
- contextual links presented after a document is closed.

The reader never becomes a full feed aggregator; it consumes a curated, limited subset for retention and knowledge purposes only.

## Symbiosis with Money Quest and Math

- **Money Quest** (finance / short decision loops on the home surface) may supply or receive progression credit when a user completes related reading or budget-oriented documents.
- **Math** (algorithmic foundations, including the Merkle chunking model) remains a first-class knowledge domain vector. Completing math-oriented material advances the corresponding vector and may unlock refined explanations of the upload integrity algorithm inside the reader.

These linkages are additive and non-destructive. They do not alter the existing Money Quest or Math surfaces; they merely create optional cross-references and shared progression accounting under user control.

## Privacy and Performance Notes

- Feed polling occurs only when the user has opted into suggestions or knowledge features, or when the home surface itself requests the feed.
- Parsed items are cached locally with appropriate expiry.
- No personally identifiable information is transmitted to feed endpoints beyond standard HTTP request headers.
